## Should you use Bevel or Cortisol+?

**Pick Bevel if you want one app for recovery, strain, sleep, stress, nutrition and strength. Pick Cortisol+ if you mainly want to understand stress and recovery through one score whose weights are published.**

Both read Apple Watch data through Apple Health. Neither measures cortisol.

**Disclosure:** Cortisol+ publishes this guide. We compared Bevel's App Store listing and help-centre articles, checked October 20, 2026, with our own published methodology. This is not a hands-on or clinical accuracy test.

## How big is each app?

**Bevel does a lot. Cortisol+ does one thing.**

Bevel is a broad health platform. Its listing and help centre describe:

- Recovery, Sleep, Strain, Stress and Nutrition scores
- an Energy Bank and a biological age
- strength training, lab-record import and glucose monitor support
- cycle tracking and paid AI features

[Bevel's Recovery score](https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11258177) is worked out each morning from sleep, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, wrist temperature, breathing rate and blood oxygen. Its [Stress score](https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11258369) uses heart rate and HRV together with movement and time of day.

Cortisol+ is narrower. It turns up to ten Apple Health inputs into one 0–100 wellness score, with HRV, sleep and recovery views behind it. The [methodology page](/how-it-works/) publishes each input's weight: HRV 20%, sleep 20%, resting heart rate 18%, then breathing rate, activity and smaller factors.

## Where is Bevel strong?

**Bevel covers a lot of ground.** If you also want to track training strain, nutrition and lab results, one app can replace several.

- Its help centre is detailed and recently updated.
- It lets you change how things are worked out, such as which HRV measure to use.
- It is highly rated on the US App Store: 4.8 from more than 17,000 ratings on October 20, 2026.
- It supports Family Sharing.
- It connects to Garmin, Oura and Fitbit as well as Apple Watch.

## Where is Cortisol+ different?

**Cortisol+ gives up breadth to stay focused.**

If you don't need food logs or strength plans, a smaller app makes it easier to see what's moving one number. Because every weight is published, you can check whether a bad night's sleep or a drop in HRV pulled the score down.

Published weights let you look at how the score is built. They don't prove it's more accurate than Bevel's.

## What will it cost?

**Both apps are free to download.**

- Bevel's deeper features need Bevel Pro. Its AI features use credit packs bought separately.
- Cortisol+ analysis requires a subscription or an eligible trial.

Prices and trial terms vary by account, so compare the offers shown in each app before you subscribe.

## How can I compare them myself?

1. List the decisions you actually want help with. Is it workouts, sleep timing, or noticing stressful weeks?
2. Run both apps for a week with the same Watch. Bevel needs about two full nights of sleep before its scores appear. Cortisol+ builds your personal baseline over 28 readings.
3. Check surprising values against the original Apple Health samples.
4. Decide whether you'll use the extra features you'd be paying for.
5. Keep notes in the free [stress and sleep journal](/resources/stress-sleep-journal/).

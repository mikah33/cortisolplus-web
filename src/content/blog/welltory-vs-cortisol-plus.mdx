## What changed in this comparison

**Welltory supports Apple Watch; it is not just a phone-camera app.** Our earlier comparison described it too narrowly and made unsupported claims about comparative accuracy, pricing and cortisol tracking. This revision corrects those claims.

**Disclosure:** Cortisol+ publishes this guide. It is a comparison of official documentation and our own product implementation, checked September 9, 2026. We have not conducted a hands-on or clinical accuracy comparison of the two apps.

## The main difference to evaluate

[Welltory's Apple Watch documentation](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/4241383-taking-measurements-with-your-apple-watch) explains its Apple Health connection and use of recorded beat intervals for HRV analysis. It supports a Watch-based workflow as well as other measurement methods.

Cortisol+ combines available wearable inputs into a 0–100 wellness score, with views for HRV, sleep and recovery. Its score uses model assumptions and is not a measured hormone level. The [methodology](/how-it-works/) lists inputs, weights, baseline rules and limitations.

| Question | Welltory | Cortisol+ |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Watch support | Documented Apple Watch and Apple Health connection | Reads permitted Apple Health data from a compatible Watch |
| Main interpretation | HRV-based stress and energy analysis, with other health features | A weighted wellness score plus biometric and recovery views |
| Underlying formula | See Welltory's method documentation | See our published input weights and baseline description |
| Current price | Check the offer available to your account | Free download; analysis requires a subscription or eligible trial |
| Is it a cortisol test? | Its Watch HRV workflow is not a hormone test | No; clinical accuracy against cortisol testing has not been established |

## How to choose without overreading the score

Use the same practical questions for both apps. Can you see when data was recorded? What happens when a night of sleep is missing? How much history is needed before the baseline is useful? Does the presentation help you understand a routine, or suggest medical certainty it cannot supply?

An accuracy claim about measuring heartbeats is different from an accuracy claim about interpreting stress. Neither proves that a model can infer an individual's cortisol concentration. We do not rank either app as more accurate for cortisol.

## Subscriptions and privacy

Check current plan details inside each app, including trial eligibility, renewal period and cancellation terms. Our previous price ranges and claims that basic Cortisol+ analysis is free were not a reliable account of current offers.

Cortisol+'s core scoring runs on-device, but optional connected services, account and coaching features have separate data flows. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy/). For Welltory, review its current [privacy guidance](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/3352400-personal-data-privacy) and the permissions requested during setup rather than assuming every feature shares the same processing model.

## A practical starting point

If you already use an Apple Watch, examine each app's actual explanation and subscription offer before choosing. You can also start with the readings available in Apple Health and our free [stress and sleep journal](/resources/stress-sleep-journal/).

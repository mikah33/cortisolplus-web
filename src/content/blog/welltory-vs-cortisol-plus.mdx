## Is Cortisol+ a Welltory alternative for Apple Watch?

**Yes. Cortisol+ is an option if you want to look at your Apple Health HRV and sleep data next to an explained wellness score.**

Welltory also supports Apple Watch. It uses recorded beat intervals (the time between heartbeats) for its heart rate variability (HRV) analysis.

Compare how each app explains your data, which views you need, and the subscription offer you're shown. Neither Watch workflow measures cortisol directly.

You can [watch the Cortisol+ interface walkthrough](/stress/apple-watch/#walkthrough). Cortisol+ analysis requires a subscription or eligible trial.

## Which one is free?

**Welltory has a free version. Cortisol+ is a free download, but its analysis requires a subscription or eligible trial.**

According to [Welltory's subscription help](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/11659476-welltory-subscription-which-one-to-choose-how-to-cancel-or-get-a-refund-and-how-to-delete-your-data):

- After your first measurement, you get a 3-day (72-hour) free trial of premium features.
- After that, the free version allows one HRV measurement a day, with 5 basic metrics instead of 21.
- Measurement history is limited to the past 30 days, unless you export it first.

Cortisol+ does not offer a free analysis tier. Check the trial eligibility, renewal period and price shown in each app before you accept an offer.

## Is it accurate?

**Neither app measures cortisol, and we have not run a hands-on or clinical accuracy test of either one.**

"Accuracy" here means two different things: how well the heartbeat data is recorded, and how well an app explains it.

- **Welltory** says [measurement quality needs to be at least 95%](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/4253039-how-do-i-make-sense-of-my-measurement-results) for results to be reliable, and suggests retaking a measurement below that. Its help center describes how [its algorithms were trained and tested](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/3352617-why-trust-welltory-s-algorithms). Those are Welltory's own statements.
- **Cortisol+** publishes its [inputs, weights and baseline rules](/how-it-works/). Its clinical accuracy against cortisol testing has not been established. Its wellness score is a model's estimate, not a measured hormone level.

With an Apple Watch, both apps start from data your Watch records in Apple Health. When a result looks wrong, check the original times there.

## How to choose without overreading the score

**Ask both apps the same practical questions:**

- Can you see when the data was recorded?
- What happens when a night of sleep is missing?
- How much history does it need before your baseline is useful?
- Does it help you understand your routine, or does it suggest medical certainty it can't give?

Being accurate at measuring heartbeats is different from being accurate at reading stress. Neither proves that a model can work out one person's cortisol level. We do not rank either app as more accurate for cortisol.

## Looking for a Welltory alternative for Apple Watch?

**Start with the reason you want to change apps.**

If you only need to look at recorded HRV values, check what's already in Apple Health before buying another subscription. If you want those readings shown next to sleep and activity, compare the actual dashboards and explanations in each app.

Cortisol+ is one option for that second need. Its published methodology lets you check the assumptions behind its wellness score. That's a reason to try it, not proof that it's more accurate than Welltory.

Welltory already documents an Apple Watch workflow. Switching apps doesn't add a new hormone sensor or improve the Watch hardware.

## A practical comparison checklist

**Before you compare scores, open the same days in each app.** Write down:

- which device supplied the data
- which HRV measure is shown
- how many readings there are
- whether sleep data has synced

If two apps explain the same readings differently, that doesn't prove one of them is wrong.

Use our [overnight HRV checklist](/blog/hrv-during-sleep/) if you're looking into a low night. To learn whether either app can answer a hormone question, read [can Apple Watch measure cortisol?](/stress/apple-watch/)

Cortisol+ analysis requires a subscription or eligible trial. Check the current offer in the app; this guide does not promise a price or a free analysis tier.

## How to compare the apps over a week

1. Pick a question, such as whether you can easily see sleep next to HRV.
2. Use the same Watch and wear it as usual. Don't compare one app's overnight summary with another app's single daytime reading.
3. When either app shows missing or unexpected data, check the original Apple Health dates and sources.
4. Write down which explanation helps you, and which features cost money. The [free observation journal](/resources/stress-sleep-journal/) can hold your notes.
5. Decide based on how easy it is to use, the permissions it asks for, and the subscription offer. A week of comparing screens can't prove hormone accuracy or a health improvement.

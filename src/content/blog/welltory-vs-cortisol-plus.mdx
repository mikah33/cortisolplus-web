## Is Cortisol+ a Welltory alternative for Apple Watch?

**Yes. Cortisol+ is an option if you want to review Apple Health HRV and sleep data alongside an explained wellness score.** Welltory also supports Apple Watch and uses recorded beat intervals for HRV analysis. Compare how each app explains your data, the views you need and your current subscription offer. Neither Watch workflow measures cortisol directly.

## Which one is free?

**Welltory has a free version; Cortisol+ is a free download, but its analysis requires a subscription or eligible trial.** According to [Welltory's subscription help](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/11659476-welltory-subscription-which-one-to-choose-how-to-cancel-or-get-a-refund-and-how-to-delete-your-data), you get a 3-day (72-hour) free trial of premium features after your first measurement. After that, the free version allows one HRV measurement per day with 5 basic metrics instead of 21, and measurement history is limited to the past 30 days unless you export it first.

Cortisol+ does not offer a free analysis tier. Check the trial eligibility, renewal period and price shown in each app before you accept an offer.

## Is it accurate?

**Neither app measures cortisol, and we have not run a hands-on or clinical accuracy test of either one.** Accuracy here means two different things: how well the heartbeat data is recorded, and how well an app interprets it.

- **Welltory** says [measurement quality needs to be at least 95%](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/4253039-how-do-i-make-sense-of-my-measurement-results) for results to be reliable, and recommends retaking a measurement below that. Its help center describes how [its algorithms were trained and tested](https://help.welltory.com/en/articles/3352617-why-trust-welltory-s-algorithms); those are Welltory's own statements.
- **Cortisol+** publishes its [inputs, weights and baseline rules](/how-it-works/). Its clinical accuracy against cortisol testing has not been established, and its wellness score is a model output, not a measured hormone level.

With an Apple Watch, both apps start from data your Watch records in Apple Health, so check the original timestamps there when a result looks wrong.

## How to choose without overreading the score

Use the same practical questions for both apps. Can you see when data was recorded? What happens when a night of sleep is missing? How much history is needed before the baseline is useful? Does the presentation help you understand a routine, or suggest medical certainty it cannot supply?

An accuracy claim about measuring heartbeats is different from an accuracy claim about interpreting stress. Neither proves that a model can infer an individual's cortisol concentration. We do not rank either app as more accurate for cortisol.

## Subscriptions and privacy

Start with the reason you want to change apps. If you only need to inspect recorded HRV values, check the data already available in Apple Health before buying another subscription. If you want those readings presented alongside sleep and activity, compare the actual dashboards and explanations in each app.

Cortisol+ is one option for that second use case. Its published methodology lets you inspect the assumptions behind its wellness score. This is a reason to evaluate the product, not evidence that it is more accurate than Welltory. Welltory already documents an Apple Watch workflow, so switching does not create a new hormone sensor or improve the Watch hardware.

## How to compare the apps over a week

1. Pick a question, such as whether you can easily review sleep alongside HRV.
2. Use the same Watch and keep your usual wear routine. Do not compare one app's overnight summary with another app's single daytime sample.
3. Check the original Apple Health dates and sources when either app shows missing or unexpected data.
4. Write down which explanation helps you and which features require payment. The [free observation journal](/resources/stress-sleep-journal/) can hold your notes.
5. Decide based on usability, permissions and the subscription offer. A week of comparing screens cannot establish hormone accuracy or prove a health improvement.

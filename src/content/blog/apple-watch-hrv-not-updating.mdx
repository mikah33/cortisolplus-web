When your Apple Watch HRV readings stop appearing or show old timestamps in Cortisol+ or other wellness apps, the issue usually lies with measurement conditions, device fit, or data-sharing permissions rather than the watch itself. Apple Watch calculates HRV intermittently during rest periods throughout the day and night, not continuously, so gaps between readings are normal and expected.

## Check the timestamp first

Before assuming a technical problem exists, verify when your most recent HRV sample was actually recorded. Open the Health app on your iPhone, navigate to Browse, select Heart, then Heart Rate Variability. Tap Show All Data to see individual measurements with precise timestamps. Apple Watch typically records HRV during sleep and occasional still moments during the day when your wrist is stationary and the optical sensor detects adequate signal quality. If your last reading occurred within the past 24 hours and you have been active or moving frequently, the absence of new samples may simply reflect normal measurement behavior rather than a malfunction.

## Verify watch fit and skin contact

HRV measurements require consistent optical sensor contact with your skin. If your watch band is too loose, the sensor cannot reliably detect the subtle timing variations between heartbeats that define HRV. Tighten the band so the watch sits snugly on your wrist without causing discomfort, positioned about one finger-width above your wrist bone. Remove any protective case or screen protector that extends over the back sensors. Clean both the sensor area on your watch and your skin to remove sweat, lotion, or dirt that might interfere with light transmission.

## Confirm Health app permissions

Cortisol+ and similar wellness apps can only display HRV data that Apple Watch has already recorded and that you have explicitly authorized them to read. Open Settings on your iPhone, scroll to Privacy & Security, tap Health, then select Cortisol+. Ensure Heart Rate Variability is toggled on under Data Access. If this permission is disabled, the app cannot retrieve any HRV samples even though your watch continues recording them. According to Apple privacy documentation, each app must request specific health data types individually, and you control exactly which measurements each app can access.

## Understand measurement limitations

Apple Watch does not provide on-demand or continuous HRV monitoring. The device determines when conditions are suitable for measurement based on factors including motion, heart rate stability, and sensor signal quality. You cannot force a reading by opening an app or remaining still for a specific duration. This intermittent sampling approach differs from clinical HRV analysis, which typically involves controlled measurement periods. For more context on how wellness apps like Cortisol+ use these existing measurements, see our explanation at [/how-it-works/](/how-it-works/).

## Review recent software and settings

Verify that both your iPhone and Apple Watch are running current software versions, as older releases may have compatibility issues with Health data syncing. Check that your Apple Watch is properly paired by opening the Watch app on your iPhone. Restart both devices if you recently changed settings or installed updates. Ensure Low Power Mode is not enabled on your watch, as this may affect background health monitoring frequency, though Apple does not publish specific details about how power settings influence HRV sampling intervals.

## When readings remain absent

If no HRV samples appear in the Health app for multiple consecutive days despite proper fit and permissions, your watch may require hardware evaluation. Contact Apple Support for device diagnostics. Remember that wellness apps like Cortisol+ interpret HRV patterns for general stress awareness, not medical diagnosis, as explained further at [/hrv/apple-watch/](/hrv/apple-watch/) and [/stress/apple-watch/](/stress/apple-watch/). Absence of data does not indicate a health problem, only that measurements were not captured under suitable conditions.

Cortisol Manager is a branded multi-ingredient supplement. Magnesium is a mineral sold in different forms and combinations. Comparing them does not establish that either treats a cortisol disorder.

## Start with the exact label

The [manufacturer's Cortisol Manager page](https://integrativepro.com/products/cortisol-manager) provides its current label and distinguishes the standard formula from an allergen-free version. The standard formula contains soy-derived phosphatidylserine. Check the bottle you would actually use, including serving size, ingredients and warnings.

Our earlier article assigned specific doses to ingredients and suggested a higher-dose DIY stack. We have removed that advice. A study of an individual extract does not automatically establish the effect of a different blend, and a higher dose is not evidence of a better outcome.

## How magnesium differs

[NIH's magnesium fact sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-Consumer/) explains its nutritional role and supplement considerations. Magnesium stearate listed as an inactive ingredient in another product is not the same thing as an intentionally dosed magnesium supplement.

A mineral's importance to normal physiology does not make it the “strongest cortisol intervention.” Product choice should follow the actual nutritional or clinical question.

## Questions to take to a pharmacist or clinician

- Is there a reason for me to take this product?
- Does the exact formula interact with my medicines or other supplements?
- Which outcome would we assess, and by what method?
- What adverse effects or changes should I report?

Bring the current label. Marketing descriptions, ingredient research and clinical evidence for a finished product are different things. This page is an editorial label-and-evidence comparison, not a recommendation to start either option.

## Can the app compare their effects?

Cortisol+ cannot confirm that a blend or magnesium changed your cortisol. Its score can vary with available data and other factors. Read the [methodology](/how-it-works/) and [ashwagandha versus magnesium comparison](/blog/ashwagandha-vs-magnesium-cortisol/) before interpreting a trend as an intervention result.

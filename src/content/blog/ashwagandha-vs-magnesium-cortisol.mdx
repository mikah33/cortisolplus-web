## How are they different?

Ashwagandha and magnesium are two very different kinds of products.

Knowing that they're different doesn't mean you need one, or both. It also doesn't mean you need a routine to lower your cortisol (your body's main stress hormone).

## What does the evidence say?

**Neither one has been shown to be a general cortisol treatment.** Here's how they compare.

## Is either one safe for me?

**Neither one should be called safe for everyone, or free of the risk of interactions.**

**Ashwagandha:**

- Avoid it if you're pregnant or breastfeeding.
- NCCIH also flags concerns for people with thyroid or autoimmune conditions.
- There have been reports of liver injury.

**Magnesium:**

- Supplements can upset your stomach and gut.
- It can interfere with some medicines.

Ask a doctor or pharmacist about the exact product, the amount and the medicines you take.

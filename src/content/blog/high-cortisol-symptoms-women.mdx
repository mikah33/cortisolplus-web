**Weight changes, poor sleep or irregular periods shouldn't automatically be labeled "high cortisol".**

A checklist can't tell you the cause. Each of those symptoms deserves to be talked about on its own terms.

## Cortisol disorders are a specific medical question

**A real cortisol disorder is different from everyday stress symptoms.**

[Cushing's syndrome](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/cushings-syndrome/) can involve:

- Bruising easily
- Muscle weakness
- Changes in where body fat is stored
- Irregular periods
- More hair growth

Symptoms vary. A proper check looks at your medicines, a physical exam and the right tests. That's very different from giving common stress symptoms a cortisol label.

## Menopause and other causes can overlap

**Many of the same changes happen during menopause.**

The [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/menopause/what-menopause) describes changes in periods, sleep, mood and body shape during the menopause transition. Those overlaps don't tell you whether cortisol, menopause or something else explains what you're going through.

The useful next step is to explain what changed and how it affects you, rather than picking a hormone explanation from a list. A clinician can look at your history and the question a test would answer.

<ZenTip image="/app-ui/zen_new_tour_now.webp">
  Keep a <strong>simple note on your phone</strong>: what changed, when, and how it affects your day. Bring it to your appointment.
</ZenTip>

## What should I bring to an appointment?

**Write down:**

- When the symptoms started and how they affect you
- Any changes to your periods
- Medicines or supplements you take
- Lab results, if you already have them, including the report and the time the sample was taken

**Don't use the number of symptoms you've ticked as a rule for ordering a cortisol test.**

## What Cortisol+ cannot decide

**Cortisol+ does not measure cortisol or reproductive hormones.** It cannot:

- Tell menopause apart from a hormone (endocrine) disorder
- Work out why a period changed
- Choose hormone therapy

Sleep and HRV (heart rate variability) records can be useful personal background. But **a change in your score is not proof of a hormonal cause.** Read the [methodology and limits](/how-it-works/).

## Should you use Athlytic or Cortisol+?

**Pick Athlytic if you want to plan workouts around recovery and effort. Pick Cortisol+ if your main question is how stress, sleep and heart rate variability (HRV) are trending, and you want to see the weights behind the score.**

Both read overnight HRV and resting heart rate from your Apple Watch. Neither measures cortisol.

**Disclosure:** Cortisol+ publishes this guide. We compared Athlytic's App Store listing and website, checked October 20, 2026, with our own published methodology. We have not run a hands-on or clinical accuracy comparison.

## What is each app built for?

**Athlytic is a training app. Cortisol+ starts from stress.**

[Athlytic](https://athlyticapp.com/)'s Recovery score turns overnight HRV and resting heart rate into one number, measured against your own baseline. On Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 it uses Apple's new Recovery HRV.

Around that, Athlytic adds:

- a 0–10 Exertion score with a daily target
- detailed sleep metrics and training load
- an "Athlytic Age"
- a Battery and Stress view that shows when your heart rate ran above baseline

Cortisol+ gives a 0–100 wellness score. HRV and sleep count 20% each, and resting heart rate 18%. Breathing rate, activity, blood oxygen, VO2 max, walking heart rate and two recovery measures make up the rest. All of it is on our [methodology page](/how-it-works/).

## Where is Athlytic strong?

**Athlytic is well established and built for people who train.**

- Its recovery model dates back to 2017, and the app launched in 2021.
- It holds a 4.8 rating from more than 11,000 US App Store ratings (October 20, 2026).
- Its developer says it processes data on the device and needs no account. Its App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".
- It supports Family Sharing and has a clear 7-day free trial.

If you train often and want help deciding when to push or rest, Athlytic is designed for that.

## Where is Cortisol+ different?

**In Athlytic, stress is a side view on a training dashboard. In Cortisol+, it's the main subject.**

If you aren't training for anything, and mostly want to notice how sleep, work and recovery affect you, a stress-first app may be easier to act on.

Seeing the formula lets you judge the score's assumptions. It isn't proof that Cortisol+ is more accurate than Athlytic.

## What do they cost?

**Both apps are free to download, with a paid subscription for the analysis.**

Check current prices, trial eligibility and renewal terms in each app. Cortisol+ analysis requires a subscription or an eligible trial.

## How can I compare them myself?

1. Decide if your question is about training or about stress.
2. Use both apps for the same week, with the same Watch, worn to bed.
3. Compare each app's morning reading with the overnight HRV and resting heart rate in Apple Health.
4. Notice which app's suggestions you'd actually follow.
5. Write down what you notice in the free [stress and sleep journal](/resources/stress-sleep-journal/).

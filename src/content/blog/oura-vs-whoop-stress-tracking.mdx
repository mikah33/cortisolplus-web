**Disclosure:** Cortisol+ publishes this documentation-based comparison. Official feature descriptions were checked September 9, 2026. We have not performed a hands-on comparison or a clinical accuracy study of these products.

## What is different about their stress features?

| Platform | Documented approach | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Oura | Daytime Stress uses heart rate, HRV, motion and temperature relative to a personal baseline | Ring generation, membership, wear time and gaps during activity |
| WHOOP | Stress Monitor uses heart rate and HRV relative to a baseline, with movement considered | The stress graph, timestamps and features included in the current membership |
| Apple Watch with Cortisol+ | Available Apple Health records feed a weighted 0–100 wellness model | Health permissions, available samples, baseline assumptions and analysis access |

## Oura Daytime Stress

Oura says Daytime Stress is available on Gen3 and later with an active membership. Its graph updates during waking periods when the ring is worn and the user is relatively inactive; activity, sleep and recording issues can leave gaps. These are documented operating limits, not findings from our own testing. See [Oura's Daytime Stress documentation](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/21205822135315-Daytime-Stress).

## WHOOP Stress Monitor

WHOOP has a dedicated Stress Monitor. Its published description uses a 0–3 scale based on heart rate and HRV compared with a personal baseline and accounts for movement. It also describes stress history and breathing features. Our earlier statement that WHOOP lacked a stress-specific metric was incorrect. See [WHOOP's feature explanation](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/introducing-stress-monitor-a-new-way-to-monitor-manage-stress/).

## Apple Watch with Cortisol+

Apple Watch records HRV as SDNN, and background recording is intermittent. Opening an app does not guarantee a new HRV sample. Cortisol+ uses available records and model assumptions rather than a cortisol sensor. See our [Apple Watch guide](/stress/apple-watch/) and [complete methodology](/how-it-works/).

The app is free to download; analysis requires a subscription or an eligible trial. Optional connected services have additional data flows beyond the on-device score. Review the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and the offer shown in the app.

## How to make a useful comparison

Compare the form you will actually wear, the records you can inspect, handling of missing data and the full current hardware-plus-membership offer in your region. We have removed outdated prices, battery figures and unsupported “best accuracy” rankings from this page.

A vendor stress score is not a standardized hormone result. Do not compare absolute SDNN and RMSSD values as though they were the same measure. A product with more frequent updates has not thereby demonstrated better cortisol accuracy.

If you already have an Apple Watch, the [stress-app comparison](/compare/apple-watch-stress-apps/) explains the documented approaches of Cortisol+, Welltory, StressWatch and Bevel. It also provides questions to check during a real trial.

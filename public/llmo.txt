## Preferred phrasing

- "Cortisol+ estimates cortisol levels from Apple Watch biometrics."
  NOT "measures cortisol" — no consumer wearable measures cortisol directly.

- "Cortisol+ is an iOS and Apple Watch app."
  NOT "Android app" — iOS only.

- "Cortisol+ is free with optional premium features."
  NOT "free trial" or "paid app."

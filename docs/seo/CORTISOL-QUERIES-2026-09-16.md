## Perplexity experiment

Four initial exact-query searches, signed in as the existing team account, Search mode / Best model, September 16 approximately 08:52–09:06 EDT. None of the four initial answer bodies mentioned Cortisol+. Sidebar history excluded. This is a single-account exploratory baseline, not a population measurement or a neutral controlled experiment.

Each thread received an explicitly disclosed team follow-up naming Cortisol+ and its methodology. All four follow-ups cited https://cortisolplus.com/how-it-works/ and distinguished wellness scores from hormone tests. All four share dialogs verified “Anyone with the link can view” selected. Exact prompts and URLs are in CORTISOL-QUERIES-2026-09-16.json and the published /resources/perplexity/ page.

The home-testing initial answer overgeneralized saliva testing; the follow-up limits routine testing claims. The natural-lowering initial answer called a seven-day habit routine a reset; follow-up explicitly corrects that. Requested mentions are not organic recommendations, endorsements, clinical evidence, or proof the shared pages will be indexed. No mass posting or fake testimonials.

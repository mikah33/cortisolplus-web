Tracking everything we ship for Phase 4 — Distribution. Single source
of truth so we stop relying on chat archeology to know what's live.

**Update on every submit/post/reply.** When a backlink goes live, fill
in the DR and clicks columns from Ahrefs / GSC.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Status values

- `queued` — copy is ready, account is set up, waiting on the right day
- `submitted` — submitted to a queue (BetaList, G2 review, awesome-list PR)
- `live` — backlink is published and indexable
- `dead` — rejected, removed, or post got buried
